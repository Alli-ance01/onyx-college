# Onyx College Portal — UI/UX direction

## Design movement

**Editorial academic journal**: a composed school portal that feels like a trusted institution, not a generic SaaS dashboard. Per the 8 October 2026 direction, the interface pairs sky-blue margins and accents (`#78c2e5`) with deep ink typography (`#183747`), chalk/paper surfaces (`#f3f7f8`), and crisp typography.

## Core principles

- **Orient before operating**: every route should establish where the user is, what matters now, and what action is next.
- **Calm density**: show real hierarchy and useful whitespace; avoid decorative cards that compete with the work.
- **Honest system feedback**: loading, empty, success, and failure states should explain what happened and what the user can do.
- **One strong next step**: actions are prioritized instead of presented as a flat button pile.
- **Respectful defaults**: accessible focus states, reduced motion support, responsive navigation, and copy that never blames the user.

## Color philosophy

- Deep ink `#183747` anchors trust, primary actions, and typography.
- Sky blue `#78c2e5` marks progress, active navigation, and the school’s signature identity.
- Chalk and paper tones (`#f3f7f8` to `#ffffff`) keep workspaces bright and legible.
- Semantic status colors (forest green, warm amber, crimson, steel blue) are reserved for system states.

## Layout paradigm

Public pages use a wide editorial frame with a split hero, pathway cards, and a restrained footer. Authenticated pages use a persistent rail, a slim contextual header, and a breathable content canvas. Cards are used as containers for a decision or workflow, not as decoration.

## Signature elements

- A compact Onyx mark built from the letter O.
- Hairline rules and ochre section markers.
- Small uppercase labels for orientation.
- Portal navigation items with compact monograms rather than noisy icon libraries.
- A subtle grain-free, paper-like background treatment made with flat color only.

## Interaction philosophy

Interactions should feel immediate and grounded: active navigation has a visible rail, buttons show progress without layout jumps, drawers close on Escape, and errors preserve user input while explaining recovery. Destructive or session-ending actions stay explicit.

## Animation

Use short opacity/translate transitions for navigation and state changes. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; no perpetual motion, gradients, glow, or novelty effects.

## Typography system

Use a high-contrast display serif for page titles and a neutral sans-serif for interface copy. Titles are tight and editorial; supporting text is readable at 1rem–1.1rem with generous line-height. Labels are compact and uppercase only when orienting the user.

## Brand essence and voice

Onyx should sound **prepared, warm, direct, and quietly authoritative**. Prefer “Review the next application” over “Manage records”. Prefer “Nothing needs your attention yet” over “No data found”.

## Wordmark/logo

Retain the existing Onyx College wordmark and amber O mark; refine its presentation with stronger spacing and contrast rather than introducing a competing logo.

## Signature brand color

Sky blue `#78c2e5` paired with deep ink `#183747`.
